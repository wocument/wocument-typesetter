# Feature opener

*Rule:* H4. Feature opener: one screen, title in the quiet zone. *Built by:* component-builder. *Status:* Approved. The owner asked that the content decides how it applies (2026-11-24).

## What it is

A feature story opens with one full screen. The lead picture fills the background. The kicker (a small label above the title), the title, the deck (one or two sentences about the story) and the credits are grouped together in the picture's quiet zone (see C2).

The story itself starts on the next screen, with a drop cap. Each feature's opening can look different, but after the opening, every feature uses the same house grid and text styles (see E10).

## Example

A full-screen photo of a house at dusk. In the calm sky at the top: "ARCHITECTURE" (kicker), "The quiet house" (title), "An architect builds a home that keeps the morning light" (deck), "By Ana Ruiz · Photographs by Lee Kim" (credits). Scroll, and the story begins with a large "T".

## The parts and how they relate

- **The parts:** kicker, title, deck (one or two sentences), credits and the lead picture; then the story starting with a drop cap.
- **Where things go:** the picture decides. The image plan records the picture's quiet zone, and the heading group sits there at every screen size (see C2).
- **How it changes with the publication's style:** a restrained publication may open with type only, on the paper colour. An expressive one may set display type over or even behind the subject of the photo.
- **Never:** put a box or tint behind the title to rescue a busy picture. Choose a different crop, or put the heading below the picture.
- **On a phone:** the opening is a cover (see C3).

## Decide from the content

Each feature's opener is designed from that feature's own brief, so no two need to look alike. Open on a picture only when the lead picture is strong and has a quiet zone for the heading group (C2). Open on type only (on the paper colour, or a colour from the story's subject) when the story is text-led, when no picture has a quiet zone, or when the profile is restrained. The title's size and treatment follow the dial (E1) and the tone: a grave subject gets a calm, simple title even in an expressive publication. The deck comes from the brief's one-sentence summary and the author's own words; never invent a claim the story does not make.

## On each screen

- Laptops and wide screens (1024 px and up): The picture fills the screen; the heading group sits in its calm third.
- Tablets (640 to 1023 px): Same, with the heading over the lower half.
- Phones (under 640 px): The picture fills the first screen with the heading at its foot; the text starts on the second screen.
