# Aesthetic profiles

The first entries of the theme catalogue. Each profile is one publication voice, described in our
own words. Profiles are never averaged: a design picks one and follows it. The rules
(`rules/`) apply inside every profile; the profile decides how they look.

`/editorial-art-direction` chooses a profile from the content brief: first the expressiveness
level (rule E1), then the profile whose **Fits** matches the content and whose **Avoid** does
not, and whose **Voice** is close to how the content is written (`reading-the-content.md`). `/publication-system` turns the chosen profile into a `.woct` theme.

Rotated (vertical) text is not in the engine. Where a source used it, set the text horizontally.

## Restrained

### Literary weekly — expressiveness: lowest
- **Grid:** 3 serif columns everywhere, never broken.
- **Type:** small heads; section names in small caps; one red accent, used rarely.
- **Pictures:** drawings, cartoons and photos sized to 1–2 columns, always rectangular.
- **Contents:** one text column: author, page number, title, italic deck. No thumbnails.
- **Voice (measured):** 19 words per sentence on average; quotes people often, uses exclamations, few numbers; American spelling.
- **Fits:** essays, reportage, fiction, criticism. **Avoid:** product, lifestyle, data-heavy pieces.

### Newspaper in a magazine — expressiveness: low
- **Density:** the densest; 2–3 column text, few pictures, small house-style charts in one colour.
- **Frame:** coloured section tabs; drawn header illustrations for named columns.
- **Emphasis:** short coloured quotes in the column alley instead of large pull quotes.
- **Voice (measured):** 23 words per sentence on average; many difficult words, rarely says "we", a wide vocabulary, few contractions (formal); British spelling.
- **Fits:** analysis, briefings, weekly round-ups. **Avoid:** visual storytelling.

### Quiet luxury — expressiveness: low
- **Mood:** calm, expensive, picture-first; the text steps back.
- **Ground:** white and light neutrals; colour comes from the photographs. A ground colour only when the story's subject owns it.
- **Type:** serif-led; each feature opener has its own display treatment (stacked serif, spaced caps, a title split across the fold); a heavier sans opener for lighter stories.
- **Pictures:** two fixed layouts: full + half + quarter page on a spread, or one picture over three quarters of the spread with one text column. Cut-out objects at the page edges with text wrapping their outline, in short items only. A photo essay with little text between long stories.
- **Ornament:** occasionally a drawn ornament taken from the subject's own decor.
- **Captions:** small sans caps, gathered per spread.
- **Voice (measured):** 18 words per sentence on average; a wide vocabulary, rarely asks questions, many semicolons (literary), few contractions (formal); American spelling.
- **Fits:** residences, portfolios, objects, sober picture-led pieces. **Avoid:** community, how-to, budget, text-heavy pieces.

## Low to middle

### Graphic news weekly — expressiveness: low to middle
- **Type:** tight classic serif body; crisp sans for all metadata; monospace for transcripts and names.
- **Motif:** the circle: folio badge, byline dot, end mark.
- **Display:** headlines as graphics: a bar-chart headline, size-balanced quote headlines, a headline split between the top and foot of a spread.
- **Colour:** black, white and one highlighter yellow for key facts and chart accents.
- **Pictures:** rectangular, often 2 columns wide and half a page high in a corner; cut-outs wrapped by contour but kept inside the grid.
- **Voice (measured):** 21 words per sentence on average; few dashes, few short sentences, almost no exclamations; American spelling.
- **Fits:** business analysis, policy, data-backed argument. **Avoid:** luxury, lifestyle, children.

### News weekly — expressiveness: low to middle
- **Frame:** small caps running head in the accent colour, then the headline.
- **Text:** intro opens in caps; sections open with bold caps run-ins; tail credit with a dash and no title.
- **Emphasis:** very large serif pull quotes in the accent colour; equal units for equal items.
- **Colour:** one accent; a one-off feature may take it from its lead photograph, a series never does.
- **Voice (measured):** 21 words per sentence on average; a narrower, repeated vocabulary, many very long sentences; American spelling.
- **Fits:** news, politics, profiles, explainers. **Avoid:** fashion, product.

### Couture classic — expressiveness: low to middle
- **Type:** serif text, light high-contrast headlines, very large serif drop caps.
- **Pictures:** staged, theatrical photo stories over many spreads; small captions in the margin; text spreads alternate with picture spreads.
- **Voice (measured):** 18 words per sentence on average; many dashes, a lot of "I" (first person, personal), many short, punchy sentences, many semicolons (literary); American spelling.
- **Fits:** fashion stories, portraits, culture profiles meant to feel timeless. **Avoid:** service, data.

## Middle

### Illustrated research journal — expressiveness: middle
- **Ground:** white body pages; tinted grounds for packages and data pages.
- **Type:** serif body in 3 columns, bold sans heads; display lettering changes per package; monospace captions and bios; statistic pull quotes with a very large number.
- **Pictures:** commissioned illustration for almost every story; portrait photos for people.
- **Data:** a front-of-book data page, a big-number page, progress bars in series.
- **Structure:** fixed front section, one large themed package, fiction and an index at the back.
- **Voice (measured):** 19 words per sentence on average; often says "we"; American spelling.
- **Fits:** explainers, science and technology long reads, research summaries. **Avoid:** luxury, lifestyle.

### Airy business monthly — expressiveness: middle
- **Space:** generous whitespace, 4 narrow columns, wide margins.
- **Display:** stacked bold serif caps title, light high-contrast caps deck, a short coloured rule, a coloured serif drop cap.
- **Pictures:** full-page commissioned illustration on feature openers.
- **Lists:** alternating cell tints instead of rules to separate entries.
- **Voice (measured):** 21 words per sentence on average; many difficult words, many very long sentences; American spelling.
- **Fits:** corporate stories, unhurried long reads. **Avoid:** dense analysis.

### Business glossy — expressiveness: middle
- **Frame:** a thick bar plus a thin rule over column heads; bold rules between sections.
- **Layout:** asymmetric, divided 2:3 both ways; mosaics of items snapped to the grid; alternating portraits in lists.
- **Display:** oversized drop caps, sometimes pale and behind the first lines.
- **Pictures:** bleeds; big portraits on colour grounds.
- **Voice (measured):** 20 words per sentence on average; many numbers and figures, few contractions (formal), rarely says "we", rarely asks questions; American spelling.
- **Fits:** rankings, profiles of people and companies. **Avoid:** investigations that need restraint.

### Founder's notebook — expressiveness: middle
- **Devices:** circled number badges, coloured numbered points, dot lead-ins, Q&A with monospace questions on lined rules, a drawn notebook edge.
- **Colour:** two warm accents on white.
- **Grid:** asymmetric; the author bio in the alley.
- **Voice (measured):** 20 words per sentence on average; talks to the reader as "you", often says "we"; American spelling.
- **Fits:** how-to, lessons, advice columns, founder stories. **Avoid:** luxury, grief.

### Men's magazine — expressiveness: middle
- **Display:** script or neon-like titles on night photography; quote-only photo spreads.
- **Text:** dense Q&A with bold questions.
- **Pictures:** dark, often black-and-white portraits; tight photo grids for city guides.
- **Voice (measured):** 17 words per sentence on average; asks many questions, short, plain words, a narrower, repeated vocabulary, a lot of "I" (first person, personal); American spelling.
- **Fits:** celebrity profiles, style, city guides. **Avoid:** service, data.

### Emphatic serif — expressiveness: middle
- **Display:** high-contrast serif headlines and decks in mixed sizes, caps and italics, key words largest.
- **Pictures:** black-and-white portraits; cut-out product collages; film-strip frames.
- **Voice (measured):** 17 words per sentence on average; many semicolons (literary), uses exclamations, many short, punchy sentences, casual contractions ("it's", "don't"); American spelling.
- **Fits:** fashion profiles, beauty and news roundups. **Avoid:** dense text.

## Middle to high

### Neo-pastel community — expressiveness: middle to high
- **Ground:** a pastel ground per department; white for features; two bright accents.
- **Type:** fixed department label and TEXT BY / PHOTOS BY pair; titles vary: run-in title and deck, or a stacked condensed title with credits in its gaps.
- **Second voice:** smaller accent-coloured sans for captions and asides, in its own narrow column.
- **Grid (measured):** 12 units, margins about 7% of the page width; body on 4 units, intros on 6, captions on 2. Large picture on one page, large picture at the top of the facing page, small one diagonally opposite, text in the remaining corner. Lead pictures near the page's own proportion; pairs repeat one proportion turned on its side.
- **Pictures:** drawn illustration for light or community content, in rows with text under each; photos for houses; data panels over pictures.
- **Voice (measured):** 21 words per sentence on average; few short sentences, rarely asks questions, often says "we", almost no exclamations; American spelling.
- **Fits:** community, renovation diaries, how-to, budget stories, makers. **Avoid:** luxury, grief.

### Elemental glamour — expressiveness: middle to high
- **Structure:** a themed series with one opener formula per item; photography coloured by the theme.
- **Display:** a giant masthead over the opening photo; caps pull quotes between bullets.
- **Voice (measured):** 18 words per sentence on average; a lot of "I" (first person, personal), plain vocabulary, casual contractions ("it's", "don't"), a narrower, repeated vocabulary; British spelling.
- **Fits:** celebrity cover packages, themed fashion stories. **Avoid:** text-led pieces.

## High

### Engineered magazine — expressiveness: high
- **Frame:** a ruled top band on every page (section, category, season) with monospace caps credits under it; short tick rules at column heads; thin column rules.
- **Grid (measured):** a half-column unit (one body column about 25% of the page width, 2.3% gutters); columns 2, 3 or 4 units wide, so pairs come out 1:1, 2:3 or 1:2; an empty channel about 7% of the page width beside the narrow outer column, mirrored on each page.
- **Type:** heavy grotesque caps headlines, serif body, monospace caps as the second voice (credits, subheads, decks, pull quotes, code).
- **Display:** block drop caps or oversized drop caps cropped by the edge; underlined heads; pull quotes with highlighted words; titles repeated and cropped at the page edges.
- **Per story:** each feature changes colour and motif on the same grid.
- **Products:** ruled catalogue cells, a coloured marker after each name, cut-outs that break their tile.
- **Voice (measured):** 19 words per sentence on average; close to the average on most measures; American spelling. (Measured from machine-read text, which turns many dashes into hyphens, so its dash count is too low and the other measures are rougher than for the other profiles.)
- **Fits:** technology features, internet culture, product roundups, interviews, investigations. **Avoid:** calm, luxury, grief, very short pieces.

### Loud glossy — expressiveness: high
- **Kit:** a reversed-out kicker slug, condensed caps headline, hairline rule, serif deck, coloured high-contrast drop caps, tail byline.
- **Text:** justified, hyphenated, indented, squared off; drop caps also mark section breaks.
- **Pictures:** collages of overlapping photos with text stepping around them; cut-outs across photo seams; timelines of cut-out figures.
- **Contents:** masthead largest, then page numbers, section, intro, credits.
- **Voice (measured):** 18 words per sentence on average; short, plain words; American spelling.
- **Fits:** celebrity, style, culture profiles. **Avoid:** data, service.

### Modern trade weekly — expressiveness: high
- **Frame:** a ruled header grid with section and date in cells.
- **Type:** all sans; monospace bylines; one bright accent.
- **Pictures:** rounded corners; big cut-outs that break the grid against a locked rectangular anchor; arrow captions.
- **Voice (measured):** 22 words per sentence on average; quotes people often, many very long sentences; American spelling.
- **Fits:** industry news, product and fashion roundups. **Avoid:** long essays.

### Zine-glossy — expressiveness: highest
- **Mood:** luxury editorial colliding with digital-native zine chaos.
- **Type:** high-contrast display serif (sometimes behind the subject); tight grotesque for front of book and statistics; small underlined kickers; 6-line drop caps; footnotes as asides.
- **Texture:** handwriting, marker circles, doodles, tape, torn paper, instant-photo frames, app screenshots, starburst badges.
- **Pictures:** seamless colour backdrops changing per spread; quiet zones for type; tilted collages in profiles, clean full bleeds in fashion. Clashing visual worlds to carry a story's tension.
- **Grid:** strict underneath: 2 wide columns for relaxed profiles, 3 narrow for service and investigation.
- **Voice (measured):** 16 words per sentence on average; a lot of "I" (first person, personal), talks to the reader as "you", asks many questions, uses exclamations; British or American spelling.
- **Fits:** young lifestyle, relationships, beauty, celebrity, service journalism. **Avoid:** grief, policy, data-heavy reports.
