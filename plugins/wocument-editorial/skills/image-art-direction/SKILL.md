# Image art direction (steps: select, place, crop)

> **Placeholder, in training.** Built during the review rounds; each round adds its lessons below and its rules to `editorial-foundations`.

## Card

| | |
|---|---|
| **Is** | The picture editor and photo director. |
| **Is not** | Changing the pacing map, theme or grid; editing pixels; writing `alt` facts the picture does not show. |
| **Prerequisites** | select: the brief. place: select and `direction.md`. crop: place, or a slot from the user. |
| **Inputs** | A picture folder (with captions or credits if any), the brief, the direction. |
| **Output** | `design/image-plan.md`: per picture, relevance with reason, quality notes, role (hero, support, detail, pause, decoration, record), scene or slot, crop as Wocument attributes per breakpoint with the focal point, draft `alt`; rejected pictures with reasons; a contact sheet. |

## Rules it applies

`editorial-foundations/references/rules/`: C (pictures), J3 (size from purpose, page to spread), J4 (a picture stays in its screen).

## Lessons from review rounds

**Round 1, style-issue study build (2026-11-26)**

- **Size from purpose first.** Decide the role, then the share of the *page*, then convert to the spread with the J3 table. A decoration (a cut-out object) that covers a quarter of a printed page is three columns at the top right of a desktop screen, an eighth of it, not eight columns.
- **A picture never crosses a screen boundary.** A tall picture beside text is cropped to the screen (`ratio`, `fit=cover`, `focus`); a picture that needs more than a screen gets a screen scene of its own. (J4)
- **Fitting lever 1 is yours.** When a scene must fit whole screens, pictures grow or shrink first, by whole baseline steps, or crop toward `focus`. Mark in the plan how far each picture may move.
- **Grids of people** (contributors, list entries): equal crops, faces in the top third (`focus="50% 25%"`), a ratio that lets the whole set fit its screen target; wide crops (2:1 or 5:2) when the screen is short.
- **Small inset portraits and signatures** are grid-placed at the head of a column, not anchored at the end of the text: an end anchor pushes them to a new screen.

**Round 1b, whole screens (2026-11-26)**

- **Spend surplus columns on pictures.** When a screen has room, promote a picture to a wider span or bleed one outer edge against an open margin. Do not give the room to wider text. (J8)
- **Never grow a picture only to fill a screen.** A picture is sized from its purpose (J3). Extra space is placed by the ladder, not absorbed by the picture.
- **Pair a large picture with a small one.** One dominant picture and one small detail, not two of equal weight. (J8)
- **A breather screen is a picture decision.** In the middle of an article, a large open area is answered by pulling a picture into its own screen, and the plan says which one.
